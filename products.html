How to Test Pure Spices at Home (Adulteration Check Guide)

Protect your family from artificial dyes and adulterated market spices. Verify the 100% purity of Raseshwari spices with these simple home tests:

🟡

Turmeric Warm Water Test

Zero Metanil Yellow Dye

Add 1 teaspoon of turmeric to a glass of lukewarm water. Pure turmeric settles slowly to the bottom leaving the water light translucent yellow. Chemically adulterated turmeric turns the water deep murky magenta or dark orange immediately.

🔴

Red Chilli Floating Test

Zero Brick Powder / Artificial Dye

Sprinkle half a teaspoon of chilli powder on the surface of water. Pure red chilli floats smoothly on top without bleeding color instantly. Brick powder or added chemical dyes sink rapidly while leaving red dye streaks.

🌿

Coriander Aroma & Essential Oil Test

Zero Sawdust / Filler Stems

Rub a pinch of coriander powder firmly between your palms. Pure stone-ground coriander immediately releases fresh citrusy essential oils and a soothing herbal aroma. Adulterated coriander feels dry like sawdust and smells dusty.

🏛️

Culinary Best Practices

How to Preserve Pure Stone-Ground Spices

Because Raseshwari spices contain zero artificial chemical preservatives, follow these simple storage tips to maintain 100% freshness and aroma.

🫙

Airtight Seal Always

Reseal our ziplock pouch tightly after every pinch or transfer to an airtight amber glass jar to shield against ambient humidity.

🥄

Use a Dry Spoon Only

Never introduce moisture into ground spices. A dry stainless steel spoon ensures the powder stays free-flowing without clumping.

❄️

Keep Away from Stove Steam

Never shake spice pouches directly over steaming cooking pots. Ambient steam degrades natural curcumin and aromatic oils.

🍳

Low-Flame Tempering (Tadka)

Cold stone-ground spices contain potent natural volatile oils. Sauté on gentle medium-low flame to release maximum fragrance without scorching.
