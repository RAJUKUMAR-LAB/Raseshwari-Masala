Why Purity Matters

Ordinary Market Spices vs. Raseshwari Pure Spices

Experience the distinct purity of traditional cold stone grinding compared to commercial mass manufacturing.

Ordinary Market Spices

Mass Commercial Powders

  • ✕ High-Heat Pulverizers: High-speed industrial mills heat spices up to 80°C, scorching natural volatile aroma oils and medicinal qualities.
  • ✕ Adulteration & Bulk Fillers: Frequently mixed with spent spices, starch, husk, or sawdust to artificially increase packet weight.
  • ✕ Synthetic Dyes & Enhancers: Artificial chemicals like Metanil Yellow or Sudan Red are often used to conceal dull, degraded ingredients.
  • ✕ Months in Warehouses: Mass-distributed stock sits in transit and warehouse racks for months, losing all fresh vitality.

Cold Stone-Ground Guarantee

  • ✓ Natural Chemical-Free Hue: Radiant golden haldi and deep crimson mirchi derived purely from natural harvest pigments, certified by FSSAI.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries regarding our cold-ground spices, WhatsApp ordering, and quality standards.

What makes Raseshwari Spices 100% pure and different from supermarket brands? ▼
Most commercial spice brands grind ingredients using high-speed industrial pulverizers where friction heats up to 80°C, causing volatile aroma oils and medicinal curcumin to evaporate. At Raseshwari Spices in Sitamarhi, we use traditional low-temperature stone milling (slow cold grinding). This keeps grinding temperatures below 38°C, sealing in natural aroma, rich flavors, and natural essential oils with zero added artificial colors, starch, or preservatives.
How does ordering directly on WhatsApp work? ▼
No debit/credit card or complicated payment gateway is needed. Just browse our spices, select your required pack sizes (50g to 1kg), and tap 'Place Order' in your cart. You can choose WhatsApp, and a neat itemized order with your delivery details is sent directly to our verified customer care helpline (+91 99055 61443 or +91 98356 54983). We verify your order and dispatch it promptly.
Which areas do you deliver to and what are the delivery charges? ▼
We deliver across Bihar, Jharkhand, and throughout India via reliable express logistics and courier partners. Orders in North Bihar enjoy rapid doorstep delivery directly from our Sitamarhi mill. Standard delivery is FREE on eligible orders.
Can restaurants, caterers, or grocery shops place bulk orders? ▼
Yes! We offer 5kg, 10kg, and 25kg commercial moisture-barrier bags with special factory-direct wholesale pricing for dhabas, restaurants, wedding caterers, and retail shop owners. You can connect directly with our wholesale manager via our bulk helpline (+91 98356 54983).
Is Raseshwari a government-registered and certified business? ▼
Yes, absolutely. Raseshwari is registered with the Government of India under Form GST REG-06 (GSTIN: 10REAPK9623A1ZS) and holds a valid Food Safety and Standards Authority of India license (FSSAI License: 20426093000164). Every order comes with an official itemized invoice.
